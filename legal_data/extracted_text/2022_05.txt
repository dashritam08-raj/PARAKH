MINISTRY OF CONSUMER AFFAIRS, FOOD AND PUBLIC DISTRIBUTION
(Department of Consumer Affairs)
NOTIFICATION
New Delhi, the 30th November, 2022

G.S.R. 859(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (1)read with clauses (j) and
(q) of sub-section (2) of section 52 of the Legal Metrology Act, 2009 (1 of 2010), the Central Government
hereby makes the following rules further to amend the Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules,
2011, namely:-

1. (1) These rules may be called the Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) (Amendment) Rules,
2022.

(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette.

2. In the Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) (Amendment) Rules, 2022, in rule 1, in sub-rule
(2), for the figures, letters and words “1* day of December, 2022”, the figures, letters and words “1* Day of
January, 2023” shall be substituted.

[F. No. WM-10/22/2021]
ANUPAM MISHRA, Jt. Secy.

Note : The Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Amendment Rules, 2022 were published in the
Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (i) vide number G.S.R. 226 (E),
dated the 28" March, 2022 and last amended vide notification number G.S.R. 747 (E), dated the
30" September, 2022.
