MINISTRY OF CONSUMER AFFAIRS, FOOD AND PUBLIC DISTRIBUTION
(Department of Consumer Affairs)
NOTIFICATION
New Delhi, the 27th April, 2026

G.S.R. 312(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (1) read with clauses (j) and (q) of
sub-section (2) of section 52 of the Legal Metrology Act, 2009 (1 of 2010), the Central Government hereby makes the
following rules further to amend the Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011, namely:-

1. Short title and commencement.- (1) These rules may be called the Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Second
Amendment Rules, 2026.

(2) They shall come into force on the 1“ day of July, 2027.
2. In the Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011, in rule 6 for sub-rule (10A), the following sub-rule

shall be substituted, namely:-

“(10A) Every e-commerce entity offering for sale any imported product shall, with effect from the Ist day of
July, 2027, ensure that the product listing of such imported product contains a searchable and sortable filter
specifying the country of origin.”.

[F. No. 1-9/5/2023-W&M]
ANUPAM MISHRA, Addl. Secy.

Note: The principal rules were published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (i) vide
number G.S.R. 202 (E), dated the 7th March, 2011 and was last amended vide number G.S.R. 128(E), dated
13" February, 2026.
