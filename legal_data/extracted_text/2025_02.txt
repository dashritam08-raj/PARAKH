MINISTRY OF CONSUMER AFFAIRS, FOOD AND PUBLIC DISTRIBUTION
(Department of Consumer Affairs)
NOTIFICATION
New Delhi, the 2nd December, 2025

G.S.R. 881(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by clauses (j) and (q) of sub-section (2) of section 52 of
the Legal Metrology Act, 2009 (1 of 2010), the Central Government hereby makes the following rules further to amend
the Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011, namely:-

1. Short title and commencement. — (1) These rules may be called the Legal Metrology (Packaged
Commodities) Second (Amendment) Rules, 2025.

(2) They shall come into force on the Ist day of February, 2026.

2. In the Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011, in rule 26 in clause (a), after the first proviso, the
following proviso shall be inserted, namely:-

"Provided further that the provisions of this clause shall not apply to pan masala.".

[F. No. I-10/4/2025-W&M]
ANUPAM MISHRA, Jt. Secy.

Note: The principal rules were published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (i) vide
number G.S.R. 202 (E), dated the 7th March, 2011 and was last amended vide number G.S.R. 778 (E), dated 23
October, 2025.
