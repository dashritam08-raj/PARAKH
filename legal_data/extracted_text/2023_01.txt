2 THE GAZETTE OF INDIA : EXTRAORDINARY [PART II—SEC. 3(i)]

MINISTRY OF CONSUMER AFFAIRS, FOOD AND PUBLIC DISTRIBUTION
(Department of Consumer Affairs)
NOTIFICATION
New Delhi, the 27th January, 2023

G.S.R. 60(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (1)read with clauses (j) and (q) of sub-
section (2) of section 52 of the Legal Metrology Act, 2009 (1 of 2010), the Central Government hereby makes the
following rules further to amend the Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011, namely:-

1. (1) These rules may be called the Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Amendment (Amendment)
Rules, 2023.

(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette.

2. In the Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) (Amendment) Rules, 2022, in rule 1, in sub-rule (2), for the
figures, letters and words “1 day of February, 2023”, the figures, letters and words “1 Day of April, 2023” shall be
substituted.

[F. No. I-10/22/2021-W&M]
ANUPAM MISHRA, Jt Secy.

Note: The Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Amendment Rules, 2022 were published in the Gazette of
India, Extraordinary, Part IL, Section 3, Sub-section (i) vide number G.S.R. 226 (E), dated the 28" March,
2022 and last amended vide notification number G.S.R. 910 (E), dated the 29" December, 2022.
