MINISTRY OF CONSUMER AFFAIRS, FOOD AND PUBLIC DISTRIBUTION
(Department of Consumer Affairs)
NOTIFICATION
New Delhi, the Sth June, 2023

G.S.R. 412(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (1), read with clauses (j) and (q) of
sub-section (2), of section 52 of the Legal Metrology Act, 2009 (1 of 2010), the Central Government hereby makes
the following rules further to amend the Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011, namely:-

1. (1) These rules may be called the Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) (Amendment) Rules, 2023.
(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette.

2. In the Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Amendment Rules, 2022, in rule 1, in sub-rule (2), for the
figures, letters and words “1 day of June, 2023”, the figures, letters and words “1* day of July, 2023” shall be
substituted.

[F. No. I-10/22/2021-WM]
NIDHI KHARE, Addl. Secy.

Note : The Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Amendment Rules, 2022 were published in the Gazette of
India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (i) vide number G.S.R. 226(E), dated the 28" March,
2022 and last amended vide notification number G.S.R. 214(E), dated the 24" March, 2023.
